## 1. Decisions Settled So Far

* **Target Financials & Pricing:** The team has established baseline targets for production and retail pricing to secure profitability, setting the selling price at 25 Euros and the production price at approximately 12.5 Euros [TS3012a: "Selling price, twenty five Euros. That's for you. The production price, twelve and a half Euros, approximately."].
* **Power Source:** Following the dismissal of solar cells, hand dynamos, and kinetic energy (due to remotes remaining stationary 99% of the time), the team settled on standard batteries [TS3012c: "Okay, will just take regular batteries."].
* **Button Layout & Power Placement:** The power button has been positioned at the very top of the interface because it is the primary function executed first [TS3012b: "power button should be on top, 'cause it's the first thing you do, turn it on."]. 
* **Core Functions & Layout:** Essential controls (power, channel selection, and volume selection) form the core of the interface, with the volume up/down controls structured in a circular layout in the center [TS3012b: "relevant buttons are the power, the channel selection and the volume selection"; TS3012b: "For up and down, ma make it a circle on it"]. 
* **Material Selection:** The exterior/buttons will utilize rubber to provide a spongy, trendy contrast inspired by fruit and vegetable trends in Paris and Milan [TS3012c: "buttons must a little bit spongy material. So ` rubber, I think ` th that's the best."].
* **Button Geometry:** The team agreed on utilizing square buttons [TS3012c: "We'll just take the take the round button, suppose. ` Uh oh the square button, sorry." / TS3012c: "Does everybody agree on that? ` Yep."].

I will beat the dolphin. No." / TS3012a: "Okay, so it's just a bee."].
* **Target Audience Clarification:** Initial assumptions that the remote's demographic targeted exclusively men were corrected to encompass both women and men [TS3012b: "is it people, okay. 'Cause I thought it was only men, so 'Kay." / TS3012b: "People, sorry. Both women and men, yeah."].
* **Energy Harvesting Alternatives Dropped:** Kinetic energy (the mechanism used by self-winding watches) and solar cells were considered and discarded because remote controls lie motionless for the vast majority of their operational life [TS3012c: "remotes lie still I think like ninety nine percent of the time. So I don't think that's a really good ` the other alternative were solar cells. I don't think that's applicable"].
* **Edition Direction:** The team shifted focus toward developing a deluxe edition to match consumer desires [TS3012c: "I think we're going towards the deluxe edition anyway, because that seems to be what the people want"].
